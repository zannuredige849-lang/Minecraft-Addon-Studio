# Pixel art rules

- Size 16, 32, or 64 — equal to the geometry texture size.
- RGBA PNG. Alpha only 0 or 255 (validator warns otherwise).
- At most 16 opaque colours per entity (validator warns otherwise).
- No anti-aliasing; outline the silhouette with a dark tone.
- Sample: [[Pixel Slime]] texture generated by `scripts/generate_sample_texture.py`.
